Our Story

A Legacy of Authentic Indian Flavors

✦

Established in 1959, Hindustan Hindu Hotel has been serving the finest Indian cuisine with an unwavering commitment to authenticity, quality, and hospitality. Our kitchen is a sanctuary where age-old recipes are preserved and celebrated.

From the aromatic kitchens of Punjab to the coastal delights of South India, every dish on our menu is a tribute to India's vast and magnificent culinary heritage.

Meet The Maestro

Chef Rajan Kumar

Executive Head Chef & Culinary Director

✦

With over 25 years of culinary mastery spanning royal kitchens across India, Chef Rajan Kumar brings the soul of authentic Indian cuisine to every plate. Trained under legendary masters in Lucknow and Punjab, his recipes carry centuries of flavor wisdom.

His philosophy is simple: "Food is love made visible." Each dish is prepared with precision, passion, and the finest handpicked ingredients.
