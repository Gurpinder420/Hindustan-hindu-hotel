FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

✦

Got questions? We've got answers. Here's everything you need to know before your visit.

Do I need a reservation to dine at Hindustan Hindu Hotel?

Walk-ins are always welcome! However, we strongly recommend reservations — especially for weekends, festivals, and groups of 4 or more — to ensure we have the perfect table ready for you. You can book via phone, WhatsApp, or by filling in the form above.

Do you offer private dining rooms for events and celebrations?

Yes! We have three private dining rooms accommodating 8–50 guests, perfect for birthday parties, anniversaries, corporate dinners, and family functions. Our event team will decorate, curate a personalized menu, and ensure a truly special experience. Contact us to check availability.

Is your kitchen fully halal certified?

Our non-vegetarian kitchen is FSSAI certified and follows strict food safety standards. We source our meats from certified halal suppliers. For specific dietary requirements, please mention them in your reservation and our chef will accommodate you with joy.

Do you have options for people with dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! We cater to a wide range of dietary needs including Jain food (no onion, no garlic), gluten-free options, low-spice preparations, diabetic-friendly dishes, and fully vegan meals. Please inform our team when making a reservation so we can prepare accordingly.

Do you offer catering services for weddings and large events?

Yes, Hindustan Hindu Hotel offers premium catering services for weddings, corporate events, puja ceremonies, birthdays, and more. We cater for groups of 50–5000 guests with live tandoor stations, sweet counters, and our full menu. Contact our catering team for a customized quote.

Is there parking available at the restaurant?

Yes! We offer complimentary valet parking for all our guests. There is also a large multi-level parking area adjacent to the restaurant. For large events, we can arrange additional parking arrangements. Please inform us in advance for groups arriving by multiple vehicles.

What is your cancellation policy for reservations?

We understand plans can change. For regular dining reservations, please notify us at least 2 hours before your booking. For private events and special bookings, we request 48 hours notice for cancellation. This helps us serve all our guests more effectively.

Do you have live music or entertainment?

Every Friday and Saturday evening, we host live Indian classical music performances — sitar, tabla, and vocal — from 7:30 PM to 10:00 PM. Special events include Qawwali nights and Bollywood acoustic evenings. Follow our social media pages for upcoming events and entertainment schedules.
